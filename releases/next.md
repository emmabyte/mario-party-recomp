- In Coin Block Blitz, the square shadows under the nine ? blocks now show on the grass, as on a console. A dark patch that came and went at the back of the field when a block was hit is gone too: it was a ring of shadow the game puts under the block and scales to hundreds of times its size, many times bigger than the whole field, and it is no longer drawn.
