Fixes for Skateboard Scamper, Piranha's Pursuit, the minigame rules screen and the Chance Time screen wipe. Like v0.2.26 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `1159bd56730094bfc71be30113e1cfc8bacf34f3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.27-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.27-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- In Skateboard Scamper in a wide window, the lava now reaches the picture's corners: there was black past its edge at the top left during READY and GO, and at the top right as the players skated on. The lava is one square the game moves along with the players, sized for a console's picture; it is now larger the wider the window is (twice its size at 16:9), its pattern keeping its size. The goal gate is also shown from further away, so its posts come in over the top of the picture rather than appearing inside it.
- In Piranha's Pursuit in a wide window, the vine stands, hanging vines, logs and boulders no longer vanish in the left side of the picture as the course scrolls past them: they go out past the window's edge.
- On a minigame's rules screen at a high resolution, the sky's clouds no longer have hard horizontal lines across them. The sky is a small picture drawn at twice its size a few rows at a time, and each strip was smoothed on its own; now the joins are smoothed across.
- At a frame rate above the game's, the Chance Time "!" screen wipe no longer shows a ring of the board around its square as it opens, or its "!" larger than it should be for a frame: the "!" now keeps step with the black around it.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions and rumble on a physical pad. Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party).
