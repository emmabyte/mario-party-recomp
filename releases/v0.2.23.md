A fix for Bobsled Run. Like v0.2.22 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `1159bd56730094bfc71be30113e1cfc8bacf34f3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.23-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.23-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- In Bobsled Run, the ice walls and the cave no longer appear all at once just ahead of the sleds, with dark blue where they were to be at the sides of the views. The game draws each stretch of the course only once a sled has come near it, which a console's narrower picture mostly hid and a wide screen shows; now the whole course is drawn in both views from the start. The dark blue above the walls is the course's sky, as on a console.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions and rumble on a physical pad. Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party).
