Fixes for thin lines under Crazy Cutter's cones, barricades and poles, for Piranha's Pursuit's ground jittering against its trees, and lines across Paddle Battle's river. Like v0.2.18 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `1159bd56730094bfc71be30113e1cfc8bacf34f3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.19-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.19-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- In Crazy Cutter, drawn above the console's resolution, a thin dark line in other colours no longer shows a little under the cones, barricades, poles and the players' drills. Their pictures sit in a sheet of pictures stacked one under another, and at an object's bottom edge the texture filter mixed in the top row of the picture under its own. A model's texture is now clamped where its part of the sheet ends.
- In Piranha's Pursuit, the dirt track and the grass and bushes behind the ferns now scroll smoothly at your display's frame rate, together with the trees and ferns. Before, they moved only 30 times a second while everything else moved every frame, so they jittered against the trees.
- In Paddle Battle, drawn above the console's resolution, the river no longer shows a thin bright line across it where one section of water meets the next. The game placed the sections a unit or two apart, so where two overlapped both showed through; they now meet edge to edge.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions and rumble on a physical pad. Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party).
