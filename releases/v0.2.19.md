A fix for the start of a minigame's rules screen in a wide window. Like v0.2.18 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `1159bd56730094bfc71be30113e1cfc8bacf34f3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.19-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.19-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- Before a minigame's rules screen, while the cloud grows out of the dark, the players and the minigame's props (Peach in the Bowser Suit, the gloves, the chests, the shells, the flags) no longer stand at the left side of a widescreen window. Only the cloud shows until it fills the screen, as on a console, and the players walk on once the sky is up, as before.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions and rumble on a physical pad. Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party).
