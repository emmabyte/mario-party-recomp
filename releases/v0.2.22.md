Fixes for the Mini-Game House and Coin Block Blitz. Like v0.2.21 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `1159bd56730094bfc71be30113e1cfc8bacf34f3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.22-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.22-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- In the Mini-Game House, the red exit door no longer slides out of its archway, with white showing beside it, as the camera turns from it to the Pot o' Skills or the Mini-Game table. The room now turns as one in the frames drawn between the game's own.
- In Coin Block Blitz, the square shadows under the nine ? blocks now show on the grass, as on a console. Before, they were missing while the players' round shadows showed: the recompiled game's renderer was stricter than the console about how close a shadow must lie to the ground under it, and the blocks' sit a little above it. A dark patch that came and went at the back of the field when a block was hit is gone too: it was a ring of shadow the game puts under the block and scales to hundreds of times its size, many times bigger than the whole field, and it is no longer drawn.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions and rumble on a physical pad. Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party).
